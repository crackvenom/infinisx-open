AI Mark Checker

Drop a picture to see whether it carries AI marks — the ones InfinisX adds to every picture it makes, and the standard ones other AI tools use. Your picture stays on your computer: it is checked here, in your browser, and never uploaded.

Getting the checkers ready…

What is checked: Content Credentials (the C2PA standard — a signed record inside the file), an invisible TrustMark watermark in the pixels (Adobe's open-source watermark, which survives JPEG saving, resizing and cropping), and China's AIGC label (GB 45438). No marks found does not prove a picture was made without AI: marks can be removed or never added.

From code, without this website: the same checker as a command-line tool and JavaScript module — github.com/crackvenom/infinisx-open/api (node check-ai-marks.mjs picture.png → JSON). A web address works when its site allows browsers to fetch it; otherwise download the picture and drop it here.

Feedback on how well the checker works (a mark it missed, a false result): open an issue. Please don't attach private pictures.

Free to use, no account, nothing stored. The watermark reader downloads its model from Adobe (about 50 MB, once). Open source: Content Credentials by the Content Authenticity Initiative (c2pa-web, MIT); TrustMark (Adobe, MIT); ONNX Runtime (Microsoft, MIT). Licences in the vendor folder. Also try contentcredentials.org/verify.
